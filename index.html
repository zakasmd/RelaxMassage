About Our Sanctuary

At Parkway Inn
The Art of Spa & Relaxation

Relax Massage & Spa at Parkway Inn invites you to embark on an exceptional wellness journey. Our center offers comprehensive, first-class amenities: an expansive indoor swimming pool, an authentic Turkish bath with heated marble, a classic Finnish sauna, custom mosaic showers, and exclusively designed VIP massage suites.

All treatments strictly adhere to the highest hygiene and sanitation standards, administered under the expert care of certified therapists. We are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to serve you at your convenience.
